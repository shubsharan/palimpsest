# Contract: Behavior Review

The behavior review is generated after attempt freeze from the durable trace, session records, frozen Git provenance, and evaluation record. It is invisible to agents.

## Required Sections

- communication use and cross-agent integration;
- negative interference and conflict recovery;
- zero or more prior-rule, contradictory-evidence, replacement, downstream-effect sequences;
- source-recognition evidence and first explicit recognition elapsed time;
- checker calls and evidence of interpreting validation as correctness;
- provider-reported token and monetary usage;
- reasoning-summary capture coverage, including captured-empty evidence;
- final canonical-origin provenance and realized team-product status.

Each observation identifies supporting trace or artifact references. Unknown or absent evidence is explicit. The record does not infer hidden reasoning.

No behavior field changes attempt validity, evaluation status, or calibration pass/fail solely because collaboration, revision, integration, recognition, checker use, or accuracy is low or absent.
